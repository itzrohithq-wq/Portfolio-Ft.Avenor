03 — Master of none

I let my desires be everywhere, scattered across the places.

I dance but wouldn't be called a dancer.
I paint but wouldn't be called a painter.
I sing but wouldn't be called a singer.
I write but wouldn't be called a writer.
I bake but wouldn't be called a baker.
I act but wouldn't be called an actor.

I let my presence be known everywhere just enough to not be terrified of the beginning but nowhere enough to make myself the best at it.

Jack of all trades, master of none.

But the quote continues; Jack of all trades, master of none, but often times, better than the master of one.

So maybe the point was never to be the best at something, but to not be scared of the single thought of starting something.

I mastered none.
But I'm free of the fear of trying something new.
And maybe that has always been the point.

So dance, because it sets your soul at peace; Paint because you find yourself in the midst of all the vibrant colours. Sing, not because you sound like glass but because you want to; Write, because the letters that leave your pen give you a sense of relief; Bake even if the cake doesn't melt in your mouth. And act because you get the privilege of seeing the world from the perspective of someone that you're not.

To create is to not let others' expertise take over the need to try.
